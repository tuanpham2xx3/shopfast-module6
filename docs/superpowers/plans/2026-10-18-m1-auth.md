# M1 Auth Vertical Slice Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Deliver register, email verification, login, refresh rotation/reuse detection, logout, current-user and role enforcement as a tested API/UI slice.

**Architecture:** Store users, refresh sessions/history and verification codes in PostgreSQL. Issue short-lived JWT access tokens and opaque rotating refresh cookies; enqueue OTP delivery through the transactional outbox.

**Tech Stack:** NestJS 10, TypeORM 0.3, PostgreSQL 16, Argon2id, JWT, React.

**Spec:** `docs/01-product-spec.md` section 3 and `docs/03-api-contract.md` section 2.

## Global Constraints

- Normalize email with trim/lowercase; passwords are 10–128 characters and never logged.
- Access tokens live 15 minutes; refresh sessions live 7 days and rotate on every refresh.
- OTP is six digits, hashed, expires after 10 minutes, allows at most five attempts, and never appears in API responses.
- Public registration creates only CUSTOMER; staff/admin are seeded.
- Cookie mutations enforce the configured Origin.

## Review Focus

- Duplicate registration and invalid login do not reveal password or stored hash.
- Refresh token reuse revokes its whole family.
- Disabled or revoked sessions cannot use access tokens.
- OTP resend invalidates previous codes and respects the 60-second interval.
- Unverified accounts cannot cross into protected business modules.
